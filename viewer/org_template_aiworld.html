Weekly history and latest-snapshot distributions for one account, coloured by its organisation type. Pick an account by clicking a table row above or searching here; the compare box on the right overlays a second account on every chart.

30-day downloads cover a rolling ~30-day window; total downloads are all-time counts, tracked from . Organisation types are self-declared on huggingface.co/organizations, captured by a single scrape () applied to every historic snapshot, so orgs that changed type, were renamed, or created later are misclassified for earlier weeks. Accounts without an org page count as “individual”. Per-account series are keyed on the account name, so a renamed account appears as two series.
